## Overview

BrandBoost operates a network of 200+ digital marketing agencies. They needed a
white-label AI solution they could offer under their own brand — content
generation, support bots, and competitive intelligence — all branded as theirs.

## The challenge

- Package Dogbro's four core services as white-label components
- Allow each agency to customize branding, pricing, and client access
- Maintain zero additional support overhead for our team

## Approach

We built a white-label SDK that wraps our core AI pipelines (content, scraping,
bots, prototyping) behind each agency's branding. Agencies configure their
custom domain, color scheme, and pricing tiers through our dashboard. The entire
platform runs on our infrastructure — they simply resell.

## Outcome

BrandBoost's 200 agencies now offer "BrandBoost AI" to their clients. Each agency
keeps 70% of the revenue; Dogbro handles all infrastructure, updates, and
back-end support. The network generates $2M/month in recurring revenue with
near-zero additional cost.
