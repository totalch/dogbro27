## Overview

Dogbro needed an identity that felt professional and innovative — a brand that
could command enterprise B2B trust while staying approachable for startups.

## The system

- **Two hues only** — navy for trust and structure, orange for energy and action
- **Clean geometric mark** — a confident, scalable symbol for any context
- **Hard-offset shadow** — a signature shadow that gives depth without blur

## Approach

Every asset derives from the same tokens, so the brand scales from a favicon to
a billboard without redrawing anything. The constraint of two hues forced
clarity in every decision.

## Outcome

A living design system that works across AI dashboards, marketing materials, and
client products — because the rules are few enough to remember.
