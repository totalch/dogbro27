## Overview

StartupLab's founders had product ideas but needed wireframes fast — for investor
pitches, hackathons, and user testing. Traditional prototyping took 1-2 weeks and
cost $5k-15k per concept.

## The challenge

- Generate high-fidelity, editable prototypes from one-line product descriptions
- Export directly to Figma/FigJam for team collaboration
- Deliver in under 2 hours, not days

## Approach

Our AI layout engine interprets product concepts and generates wireframes with
proper component hierarchy. The output maps to Figma components via our vector
mapping pipeline, producing fully editable frames that designers can refine.

## Outcome

StartupLab cut prototype time from 2 weeks to 2 hours, reducing costs from $10k
to $10 per prototype. They've validated 30+ product ideas using our system,
with 5 startups securing seed funding based on the prototypes.
