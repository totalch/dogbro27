## Overview

Nexus Capital needed real-time competitor intelligence across 500+ startups in
their portfolio companies' verticals. Manual research cost them $10k/month and
still missed market moves.

## The challenge

- Scrape and analyze public data from competitor websites, funding announcements,
  and social signals
- Deliver actionable insights in a visual dashboard
- Keep compute costs under $150/month

## Approach

We built an automated pipeline that scrapes public sources using rotating proxies,
processes raw data through our AI analysis engine, and delivers structured
visualizations to a dashboard. The AI surfaces trends, funding shifts, and
product launches — all updated daily.

## Outcome

Nexus reduced manual research spend from $10k/month to $95. They now get
automated alerts on competitor moves within 24 hours, directly in their Slack
channel.
