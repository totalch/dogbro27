At Dogbro, we train our content generation models exclusively on copyright-free
public domain data. Here's why this approach is critical for enterprise adoption.

## The copyright problem

Most AI content tools train on scraped internet content — which means they risk
generating text that infringes on existing copyrights. For enterprises, this is a
non-starter.

## Our solution

We use only:
- Wikipedia (CC BY-SA)
- Common Crawl (public domain)
- Public domain literature and government documents

This ensures every piece of content we generate is free of copyright risk.

## The business case

By using public domain data and fine-tuning open-source models (like Llama 3 and
Mistral), our inference costs are 80% lower than GPT-4-based competitors. Our
clients get enterprise-grade AI content without the legal headaches.

> Zero copyright risk. 80% lower costs. That's the Dogbro difference.
